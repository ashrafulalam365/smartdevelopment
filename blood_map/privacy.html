Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 20, 2026

Welcome to Blood Map. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

To provide a better experience and facilitate blood donation, we collect the following personal information:

  • Full Name: To identify users within the community.
  • Phone Number: To verify your account and allow seekers to contact you.
  • Blood Group: Essential for matching donors with recipients.
  • Location: Used to show nearby donors to those in need (Division, District, Upazila).
  • Password: For secure login to your account.

2. How We Use Your Information

The primary purpose of collecting your data is to connect blood donors with those who need blood. Specifically:

  • To list you as a potential donor based on your blood group and location.
  • To allow other users to contact you via call or in-app messaging for urgent blood needs.
  • To provide notifications regarding join requests or messages.

3. Data Security and Encryption

We take the security of your data very seriously.

  • Password Protection: Your password is never stored in plain text. We use advanced SHA-256 Hashing with Salt encryption to ensure your login credentials remain secure and inaccessible to anyone.
  • Cloud Security: We use Google Firebase to store data, which employs industry-standard security protocols.

4. Data Sharing and Third Parties

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. However, we use some trusted third-party services that may collect information used to identify you:

  • Google AdMob: To show advertisements within the app.
  • Firebase Analytics: To monitor and analyze app usage to improve our service.

5. User Rights (Update & Delete)

We believe you should have full control over your data:

  • Edit Information: You can update your name, location, blood group, and social links anytime from the "Profile" section.
  • Account Deletion: You have the right to permanently delete your account at any time. Upon deletion, all your personal data, including profile info, messages, and groups, will be permanently removed from our servers.

6. Children's Privacy

Our service does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.
