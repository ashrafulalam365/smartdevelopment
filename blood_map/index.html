Blood Map - Connect & Save Lives

A voluntary blood donation platform connecting donors and recipients based on location. Search nearby donors by Division, District, and Upazila easily.

Download App on Play Store
Important Notice: Blood Map strictly facilitates voluntary and unpaid blood donations. In case of critical medical emergencies, users are advised to rely on professional emergency services and official hospital blood banks.

Key Platform Features

📍 Location Based Search

Filter voluntary donors directly by Division, District, and Upazila for fast local response.

📞 Direct Communication

Contact potential donors via direct phone call or secure in-app messaging when urgent.

🔒 Data Protection

Passwords encrypted using advanced SHA-256 Hashing with Salt to keep your account safe.
